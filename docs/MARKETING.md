# Store Canary Marketing

This document is the repository source of truth for public marketing claims. `PROJECT_STATUS.md` controls current operational status, `ARCHITECTURE.md` controls the application boundary, and public copy must remain consistent with both.

## ICP

Store owners and WooCommerce operators who want fewer operational surprises and less dashboard patrol.

Do not add assumptions about company size, order volume, geography, industry, revenue, budget, or technical maturity without verified evidence.

## Problem

Important WooCommerce exceptions can hide across routine operational screens until they consume operator time or affect a customer. The specific problems Store Canary may name are:

- stuck paid orders;
- failed payments;
- new stockouts; and
- broken scheduled actions.

The marketing problem is repetitive dashboard checking and delayed awareness, not a claim that every store experiences every exception.

## One-line offer

WooCommerce Daily Ops finds operational exceptions and turns them into one concise, actionable daily digest.

The approved headline is: “The quiet morning check for a busy WooCommerce store.”

## How it works

1. Daily Ops checks for the supported WooCommerce operational exceptions.
2. It presents the exceptions in one concise daily digest so the operator can decide what needs attention.
3. Access begins with a guided installation on a backed-up staging site before production.
4. The product is local-first and does not send customer-data telemetry.

Do not describe a cloud dashboard, data pipeline, automation, remediation, or integration that the product and repository do not support.

## Request path

The primary CTA moves the visitor to the access section. The email CTA opens the existing structured `mailto:` request to `homer.agent.erik@gmail.com`. Its template asks for:

- store URL;
- the requester's role;
- the operational problem they want the product to solve; and
- WooCommerce version, if known.

The site does not submit a form or collect the request. The visitor's mail application sends the email.

The CTA and address must remain unchanged until the owner completes the Google Workspace secondary-domain and authenticated mail-DNS work and explicitly approves the update. That work is owner-gated and is not represented as complete.

## Path to first dollar

The current site supports acquisition and qualification, not a payment transaction:

1. A qualified WooCommerce operator finds the public explanation.
2. The operator requests access through the structured email.
3. Compatibility and the appropriate installation path are confirmed.
4. Installation is guided on a backed-up staging site before production.
5. Any commercial terms and payment collection would require a separate owner-approved step outside this site.

No payment is taken by this site, and marketing must not imply that a paid customer or revenue already exists.
